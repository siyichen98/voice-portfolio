A CASTING DECISION

Finding the narrator

Retrospective account by Siyi Chen

  1. 01 / CLIENT REVISION

    The brief changed from a male to a female narrator, while retaining a warm, calm storytelling style.

  2. 02 / WHAT I LISTENED FOR

    A mature-sounding voice, an unhurried pace and a warm tone without a sharp or shrill edge.

  3. 03 / WHY THIS VOICE

    Other candidates sounded too young, rushed, overly promotional or more emotionally heightened than the role required. The selected voice felt steady and reassuring.

Scene excerpt · 0:54. Character voices and audio post-production by Siyi Chen.

Directing an emotional turn

The sisters’ conversation moves from fear and suspicion toward openness and reconciliation. The producer’s feedback on the initial version was that it sounded like two people reading lines.

I broke the dialogue into performance beats and adjusted volume, pace and delivery rather than relying only on broad emotion labels. I created and tuned both character voices, edited the dialogue, synchronized it to picture and completed the mix.
